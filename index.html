Anuran distribution and richness across an altitudinal gradient in montane forests

undergraduate research project (spring 2023)

As Neotropical communities rapidly respond to environmental and anthropogenic pressures, measuring species richness and abundance across elevational gradients provides insight into the health of the ecosystem. In this study, Anuran surveys were conducted across four of the seven distinct Holdridge life zones in Monteverde, Costa Rica, to compare with a corresponding study in 2019. Visual encounter surveys (VES) and audio encounter surveys (AES) methodologies were used at five non-continuous locations.
